Questions

How do I message a number without saving it?

Install DialToChat, pick the number from your call log, tap it — a WhatsApp chat opens without adding a contact.

Why isn’t it on Google Play?

The app reads your call log to list recent numbers — a permission Google Play restricts. So it’s a direct APK download from this site.

Is it safe?

Yes. Your call log is read only on your phone to build the list. Nothing is uploaded; no data leaves your device.

Which phones?

Android 8 and newer. WhatsApp must be installed.
